ABOUT GREENINK ACADEMY

Where Expert Guidance Meets Smart Technology

Founded in 2019 in collaboration with Mahendras, GreenInk Academy began as a high-performing hybrid institute and has evolved into a technology-driven online academy built for results.

At GreenInk, learning is structured for success—from well-planned live classes to mentorship by top ex-government officials such as Dr. Sylendra Babu IPS (Ex-DGP), 10+ serving officers, and 100+ subject experts across disciplines.

Powered by an AI-driven LMS that tracks progress and builds confidence, GreenInk ensures the right methodology, consistent practice, and predictable success.
